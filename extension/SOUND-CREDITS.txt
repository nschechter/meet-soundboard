These nine recordings keep their original pitch. Opening silence is
trimmed from the applause, boom, tomato, crickets, laugh track,
I'll be back, and Luigi clips.
Crickets has a 1.8x volume boost. Mono WAV conversion and peak limiting
keep them ready for playback without changing them into new sounds.

The other six effects below use CC0 1.0 Universal.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Their audio uses public high-quality MP3 previews with short excerpts,
mono conversion, level adjustment, edge fades, and MP3 encoding.
BOOOOO! uses light compression.
These six effects use a 35 Hz high-pass filter.
