The bundled audio uses the public high-quality MP3 previews. Changes include
short excerpts, mono conversion, level adjustment, edge fades, and MP3 encoding.
Crickets uses a low-frequency filter. BOOOOO! uses light compression and a
louder level. Egg attack layers egg-crack foley with Cartoon Splat. Tomato
splat uses Cartoon Splat. Both splat effects repeat three hits with small
pitch changes. Source credits for these layers are included below.
All clips use a 35 Hz high-pass filter. No Discord audio or branding is included.
