# Store listing draft

Name: Meet Soundboard

Short description: A soundboard inside Google Meet. Play classic reaction sounds or your own clips into your meeting audio.

## Description

Add a little personality to your Google Meet calls. Open the soundboard in Meet and play a classic reaction or a clip from your own collection. No screen sharing or extra audio driver is needed.

Includes 15 reactions such as an air horn, applause, rimshot, correct-answer ding, crowd boo, record scratch, and sad trombone. Preview clips, change effect volume, include or exclude your voice, and stop playback with one button.

Custom sounds stay in local extension storage. Previewing or playing a custom sound passes it into the Meet page. Playing to the call sends it to participants through Meet. The extension does not record meetings or send audio to the developer.

Keep Meet's microphone on to send effects. Turn off noise cancellation or Studio sound if it removes them. Use headphones for previews. This extension works on the Google Meet website and may need updates when Meet changes.

Meet Soundboard is an independent project. It is not made or endorsed by Google or Discord.

## Permission explanations

`storage` saves custom clips and playback preferences locally.

Content scripts on `https://meet.google.com/*` display the panel and mix chosen effects into the existing outgoing audio stream. No other sites are included.
